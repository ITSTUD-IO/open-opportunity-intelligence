# Product Vision

## Problem
Engineering consultancies struggle to identify companies with emerging software engineering needs before a formal RFP. Job ads and company announcements are fragmented and do not directly express purchasing intent.

## Vision
Open Opportunity Intelligence turns legally accessible public market signals into explainable, evidence-backed hypotheses about potential engineering opportunities.

## Initial users
Internal business development, solution architects and consulting leadership. The first deployment is an internal tool, while the software is open source.

## Core workflow
1. Collect job postings and company announcements from permitted sources.
2. Resolve company identities, normalize records and track changes over time.
3. Detect signals for platform engineering, DevOps, cloud modernization, software architecture and AI engineering.
4. Correlate signals into opportunity hypotheses with citations, dates, confidence and a separate business-priority score.
5. Review, dismiss or qualify opportunities and record feedback.

## Non-goals for MVP
- Comprehensive coverage of every job board
- Automated unsolicited outreach
- CRM replacement
- Multi-tenant SaaS, billing or enterprise identity management
- Treating hiring activity as proof of purchase intent

## Principles
Evidence first; explicit uncertainty; human review; source licensing and privacy by design; replaceable data sources and LLMs; no publication of internal lead data.
